# FolioFlux

**The AI finance workspace for freelancers, small teams and their accountants. Every number, traceable.**

Bring your bank and card exports. FolioFlux builds a ledger you can verify, where every figure opens the rows behind it.

- 🌐 [folioflux.com](https://www.folioflux.com), free in early access
- 💼 [FolioFlux on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/folioflux)
- 👤 Built by [Andrii Furmanets](https://github.com/afurm), Founder & CEO
